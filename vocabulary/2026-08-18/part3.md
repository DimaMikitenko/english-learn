## Task
1. Я люблю кататися на велосипеді щовихідних.
2. Учора інструктор прийшов до мене і запропонував нову активність.
3. Я провів два роки, вивчаючи англійську, перш ніж полетів у відпустку.
4. Після довгого дня я відчуваю себе втомленим.
5. Мій перший політ на параплані був у Грузії.

## My Answers
1. I like biking every weekend.
2. My instructor came to me yesterday and suggested a new activity.
3. I spent 2 years learning English before flying on vacation.
4. I feel tired after a long working day.
5. There's my first flight on a paraglider in Georgia.

## Summary
1. Correct — good use of "-ing" after "like."
2. Correct — good past tense and "suggest X to someone."
3. Minor: "I spent two years learning English before going on vacation." "Go on vacation" is the natural collocation; "fly on vacation" isn't standard.
4. Correct — nice "feel" + adjective.
5. "My first flight on a paraglider was in Georgia." "There's" isn't right for stating a specific fact about yourself — use subject + verb.

Two new minor patterns logged: the "go on vacation" collocation and misusing "there's" for personal facts. Everything else — "I like + -ing" and "feel + adjective" — is solid now.
