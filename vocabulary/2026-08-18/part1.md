## Task
1. Зазвичай я п'ю каву вранці, перед роботою.
2. Минулого літа ми їздили у відпустку до моря.
3. Мені подобається читати книги перед сном.
4. Він живе в цьому місті вже п'ять років.
5. Ми плануємо зустрітися з друзями у суботу.

## My Answers
1. I usually have a coffee in the morning, before I start to work.
2. We went on vacation to the sea last summer.
3. I like to read books before going to bed.
4. He's lived in this city for five years.
5. We're going to meet with friends on Saturday.

## Summary
1. Correct. A more natural short version: "before work."
2. Correct — and successfully kept "vacation" (a past recurring mix-up), good progress! "To the seaside" is a more idiomatic alternative to "to the sea."
3. Correct. Since it describes a habit, "I like reading books" (-ing form) sounds slightly more natural than "to read," but both are fine.
4. Correct — good use of "for" + duration.
5. Correct. Minor style note: "meet friends" (without "with") is more casual/natural for social plans; "meet with" leans more formal/business-like.

No grammar errors this round.
