## Task
1. Учора я ходив у магазин, щоб купити хліб.
2. Вона працює в офісі вже три роки.
3. Ми плануємо поїхати на відпочинок наступного місяця.
4. Мені подобається слухати музику, коли я готую.
5. Він забув свій телефон удома цього ранку.

## My Answers
1. Yesterday I went to the store to buy some bread.
2. She's already been working in the office for three years.
3. We're going to go relax next month.
4. I'd like to listen to music while I'm cooking.
5. He forgot his phone at home this morning.

## Summary
1. ✅ Correct, natural.
2. ✅ Correct! Present perfect continuous works nicely here with "already."
3. ⚠️ Better: "We're planning to take a vacation next month." — "Відпочинок" specifically means "vacation/rest trip"; the original translation lost that meaning, and "go relax" sounds too casual/vague for this context.
4. ⚠️ Better: "I like listening to music while I cook." — "Мені подобається" = a general habit/preference ("I like"), not a conditional wish ("I'd like" = "I would like," used for a specific request right now). Also, after "like," use the "-ing" form for general habits.
5. ✅ Correct.

Score: 3/5 fully correct.
