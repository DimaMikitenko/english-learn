## Task
1. Я, безумовно, погоджуся допомогти тобі із цим проєктом.
2. Раніше вона пропонувала мені інший маршрут подорожі.
3. Він проводить більше часу катаючись на велосипеді, ніж дивлячись телевізор.
4. Це допомагає мені почуватися впевненим перед іспитом.
5. Мій друг порадив мені спробувати нову каву в цьому кафе.

## My Answers
1. I definitely agree to help you with this project.
2. She previously suggested another route to walk.
3. He has spent more time biking than watching TV.
4. It helps me feel better before an exam.
5. My friend has suggested me trying a new coffee in this cafe.

## Summary
1. ✅ Correct — good use of "definitely."
2. → "She previously suggested another travel route to me." Dropped "to me" — "suggest X to someone." Also "маршрут подорожі" = "travel route," not "route to walk."
3. → "He spends more time biking than watching TV." Simple present fits better for a habitual pattern than present perfect.
4. → "It helps me feel confident before an exam." "впевненим" = "confident" — "better" loses the specific meaning.
5. → "My friend suggested that I try new coffee at this café." "suggest someone trying" isn't valid — use "suggest (that) someone do something." Simple past also fits better than present perfect.
