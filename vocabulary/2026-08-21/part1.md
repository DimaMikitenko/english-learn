## Task
1. Зазвичай я люблю кататися на велосипеді вранці, це допомагає мені почуватися розслабленим.
2. Минулого літа я вперше літав на параплані в Грузії, і це було дивовижно.
3. Я витрачаю більше часу граючи з дітьми, ніж читаючи книги.
4. Мій інструктор порадив мені спробувати ще раз, і я погодився.
5. Це був звичайний день з моєю звичною рутиною, нічого особливого.

## My Answers
1. Usually I love biking in the mornings. It helps me to feel relaxing.
2. The first time I flew on a paraglider was last summer in Georgia, and it was amazing.
3. I spend more time playing with the kids than reading books.
4. My instructor had suggested that I try again, and I agreed.
5. It was a normal day with my daily routine, nothing special.

## Summary
1. Only issue: "feel relaxing" → **"feel relaxed."** After "feel," use the adjective form, not "-ing." (Recurring pattern — 3rd time now.)
2. ✅ Correct — good use of "flew" and "paraglider."
3. ✅ Correct — nice parallel "-ing" structure after "than."
4. ✅ Grammatically fine. Style note: simple past ("suggested... and I agreed") is more natural here than past perfect ("had suggested"), since these are just two sequential past events rather than one happening before another already-past event.
5. ✅ Correct — good fix on "normal" vs "common" from before.
